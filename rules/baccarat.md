# Baccarat

Baccarat looks intimidating and is the simplest game in this collection. Two hands are dealt - one called Player and one called Banker - and you bet on which will come closer to 9. Nobody makes any decisions after the bet; every card that comes out is dictated by fixed rules. A hand takes under a minute.

## What You Need
- **Players:** 1-14 at a full table, but you are only ever betting, never playing a hand.
- **Deck:** 6 or 8 standard decks shuffled together.
- **Extras:** Chips to bet with.

## Key Terms
- **Player and Banker:** The names of the two hands on the table. They are not people - you may bet on either one regardless of where you sit.
- **Natural:** A two-card total of 8 or 9. It ends the hand immediately.
- **Third-card rules:** The fixed table that decides whether each hand draws one more card.
- **Push:** A tie, where Player and Banker bets are returned.
- **Commission:** The 5% the house takes from winning Banker bets.

## Setup
1. Place a bet on Player, on Banker, or on a Tie before the deal.
2. The dealer deals two cards to the Player hand and two to the Banker hand, all face up.

## How to Play
Card values are unusual: aces are 1, cards 2 through 9 are face value, and 10s and all face cards are worth **zero**.

Only the last digit of a total counts. A 7 and an 8 add to 15, which is a hand worth 5.

The rest is automatic:

1. If either hand totals 8 or 9, that is a natural. Both hands stand and the hand is over.
2. Otherwise the **Player hand** draws a third card on a total of 0-5 and stands on 6 or 7.
3. The **Banker hand** then draws according to the fixed table below, based on its own total and the value of the Player's third card.
4. Nobody chooses anything. The dealer simply follows the chart.

If the Player hand stood on 6 or 7, the Banker simply draws on 0-5 and stands on 6 or 7. Otherwise:

| Banker total | Draws when Player's third card is | Stands when it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0, 1, 2 | anything | never stands |
| 3 | 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9 | 8 |
| 4 | 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 | 0, 1, 8, 9 |
| 5 | 4, 5, 6, 7 | 0, 1, 2, 3, 8, 9 |
| 6 | 6, 7 | everything else |
| 7 | never draws | always stands |

## Scoring & Winning
- The hand closer to 9 wins.
- **Player bets** pay even money - bet 10 and win 10.
- **Banker bets** pay even money minus 5% commission, because the Banker hand wins slightly more often.
- **Tie bets** typically pay 8 to 1, and both other bets push.

## Example
The Player hand is dealt 7♥ and 2♣ for a total of 9. That is a natural, the hand ends instantly, and anyone who bet on Player wins.

## Variations
- **Punto Banco:** the version above, and the one found in nearly every casino.
- **Chemin de Fer:** the older French game where players take turns being the bank and actually choose whether to draw.
- **Mini-baccarat:** a smaller, faster table with lower stakes and a single dealer.

## Tips for New Players
- Banker is the best bet available even after the commission. Player is close behind.
- The Tie bet looks tempting at 8 to 1 but has by far the worst odds on the table.
- Scoreboards tracking previous results are common at baccarat tables. They have no predictive value - every hand is independent.
