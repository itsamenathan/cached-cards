# Speed

Speed (also called Spit) has no turns - both players play at the same time, as fast as they can, until one of them is out of cards. You play a card that is one rank above or below whatever is showing in the middle. A game lasts about 3 minutes and is usually played best of several.

## What You Need
- **Players:** Exactly 2.
- **Deck:** One standard 52-card deck. Suits do not matter at all.
- **Extras:** A table with room for six piles.

## Key Terms
- **Draw pile:** Your personal face-down stack of 15 cards.
- **Hand:** The 5 cards you hold and play from.
- **Replacement piles:** Two 5-card stacks between the players, used to break a deadlock.
- **Play piles:** The two face-up cards in the very middle that everyone plays onto.
- **In sequence:** One rank higher or lower than the card showing. Suit is irrelevant.

## Setup
1. Split the deck and deal each player 20 cards: 15 face down as their draw pile, and 5 in hand.
2. Place the remaining 12 cards as two replacement piles of 5, one on each side of the middle, and 2 single cards face down in the centre between them.
3. When both players are ready, flip the two centre cards face up at the same time. Play begins immediately.

## How to Play
There are no turns. Both players play whenever they can:

1. Play a card from your hand onto either centre pile if it is **one rank higher or lower** than the card showing there. A 7 accepts a 6 or an 8.
2. Aces wrap around, so a king can go on an ace and an ace can go on a king.
3. Immediately draw from your draw pile to bring your hand back up to 5 cards. You may only hold 5 at a time.
4. If both players get completely stuck, both say so, then each flips the top card of their replacement pile onto their centre pile at the same time. Play restarts.

## Scoring & Winning
- The first player to get rid of both their draw pile and their hand wins the round.
- There is no scoring beyond that. Most people play a set of rounds and count wins.
- If both players are stuck and the replacement piles are gone, shuffle the centre piles, split them into new replacement piles, and continue.

## Example
The centre card is a 7 and you hold a 6. You slap it down, immediately draw a replacement card, and look for the next play while your opponent is still deciding.

## Variations
- **Spit:** the more common name outside the US, with slightly different pile sizes and a scramble for the smaller pile at the end.
- **California Speed:** cards are laid out in a grid and players slap matching pairs instead.
- **Three or four players:** possible with a second deck and extra centre piles.

## Tips for New Players
- Play with both hands. Nothing stops you.
- Keep your five hand cards spread out and facing you so you can see every option at a glance.
- Watch both centre piles, not one. Beginners fixate on the pile in front of them and miss easy plays.
